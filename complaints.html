Game complaint service

Withdrawals stuck, a bonus that never arrived, or a link that leads nowhere? Tell us what happened. The service is free, and every case is recorded.

Step 1

Contact the operator first

Open the app's own help centre and raise a ticket from your registered account. Note the ticket number — we cannot move a case forward without it.

Step 2

Collect your evidence

Screenshots of the transaction, your balance, the offer you accepted and any chat with support. Dates and times make the case much easier to follow.

Step 3

Send it to us

Use the form below. We acknowledge within two working days, contact the operator through our channel and follow up for up to 14 days.

File a complaint

Fields marked * are required. Submitting opens your email app with the details filled in, so you keep a copy of what you sent.

Prefer email? Write to support@yonodiwagames.online. We never ask for account passwords or one-time codes.

Before you write

Keep the message factual: what happened, when, what you want resolved. Threats or abusive messages close tickets faster than they open them.
