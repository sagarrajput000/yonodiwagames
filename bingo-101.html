About BINGO 101

BINGO 101 is the directory's bingo-first Yono listing, with a name that reads like an introductory edition.

Bingo apps usually organise play around rooms, tickets and scheduled draws. The 101 may suggest a beginner-friendly entry point, but the actual room formats and prices are controlled by the operator.

The listing is live in the Yono collection. It is a directory entry, not a review of any particular room or prize pool.

What a bingo listing involves

Bingo is a chance-based game built around tickets and draws. Before joining a room, check the ticket price, the prize split, the draw schedule and whether the room is free or real-money.

The 101 positioning

An introductory name can mean simpler navigation or lower-stake rooms, but it is still branding. Read the room rules rather than assuming the app is risk-free or beginner-only. You can compare it with other Yono apps in the directory.

Before you buy a ticket

Confirm the total cost, the time of the draw and the withdrawal rules for any winnings. Keep your ticket confirmation and do not share account credentials or OTPs.
