Why this directory exists

New Yono and Diwa apps appear every week. Most lists you find online are either out of date or built only to push a link. YonoDiwaGames keeps one clean page per game: what it is, where it links, whether it is live and what score it earned.

The directory is maintained by hand. Listings are added from a private admin panel, reviewed before they go public, and removed when a link stops resolving.

How we score games

Every listing carries an editor score out of 10. The number is a shortlisting aid, not a promise about payouts.

  • Listing quality — clear name, artwork, description and a valid referral link.
  • Link stability — how reliably the link opens on mobile and desktop.
  • Complaint history — what visitors report and how quickly the operator responds.
  • Freshness — whether offers and details are kept up to date.

We do not accept payment for a score. A game cannot buy a higher position in the directory.

Complaints, honestly

We are a directory, not a regulator. We cannot freeze an account, release a withdrawal or force an operator to pay. What we can do is document the case, pass it to the operator contact we hold and follow up for up to 14 days.

If your case needs legal or regulatory action, contact the licensing authority named in the operator's own terms.
